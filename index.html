Chess Lab

Import your chess.com games and see where your rating goes.

Enter a username and press Import games. Games come from chess.com's public API, so no password is needed. Stats are worked out in your browser, and Stockfish runs on your own device to explain each mistake and turn it into a puzzle you can practise.

No chess.com account? You can still play Stockfish and learn as you go: it stops you when you make a mistake.
